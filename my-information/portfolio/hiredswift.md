My role. Fullstack Lead Developer

# Project description.

Job hunting eats time. Each application can take 30 minutes or more, and the sheer volume wears people down before they reach an interview. HiredSwift is a human-powered service that takes that work off their plate. Users buy an application package and upload their documents, then a real team applies on their behalf using personalized, human-written materials tailored to each role, not auto-generated spam. Applicants reclaim hours, feel far less pressure, and move through the hiring process faster while they focus on preparing for interviews.
