# Project description.

Many people want to get fit but do not know how to structure workouts or eat well, and motivation fades fast. Fitdyz AI is a mobile app that solves this. Users enter their details and the app builds a personalized workout and diet plan, then tracks calories, workouts, and meals in one place. An AI Fitbot, grounded in each user's plan through a retrieval pipeline over a vector database, answers questions in context, while challenges reward tokens that users spend to chat with a real coach. The outcome is a clear plan, steady motivation, and fitness that is easier to stick with.
