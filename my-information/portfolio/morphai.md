Morph AI — Multi-Agent AI application
My role. Project Lead Developer and Owner

# Project description.

Power users juggle several AI subscriptions and apps, switching tools for writing, research, and creative work. Morph AI brings them into one mobile app under a single subscription. Behind it, an orchestration layer routes each request to the model or agent best suited to the task, drawing on multiple leading LLMs and specialized agents so users never pick or manage models themselves. The result is one place for productivity, research, and creative work, with one bill instead of many and no app switching.
