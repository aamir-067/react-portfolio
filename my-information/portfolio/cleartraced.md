# Project description.

Banks waste weeks pulling ESG and financial figures from company reports by hand. Cleartraced automates it. For a given company and year, crawler agents find the right disclosures, from annual reports to sustainability statements, then cross-check them for consistency. Extractor agents pull the exact data points each client needs, from revenue and EBIT to carbon emissions and board makeup, across 300+ fields. A multi-layer algorithm scores each value for confidence and an analyst verifies it, keeping every figure traceable to its source. Clients include Millistream and Global Child Forum.
