My role. Full stack Engineer

# Project description.

Product photoshoots are expensive and slow, which holds back brands that need fresh visuals often. LookAtlas is an AI studio that removes that cost. Users upload product images, pick a style or pose, and the platform generates clean, studio-quality photos and short videos in minutes, handling lighting, angles, and composition automatically. Brands skip the studio, the crew, and the scheduling, turning a costly, time-consuming shoot into a few minutes of work while keeping a consistent look across their catalog.
